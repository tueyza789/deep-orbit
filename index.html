Deep Orbit

Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026

This app has no account, no server of its own, and no analytics. It does not collect, transmit, or sell anything about you. Everything it knows stays on your device, and deleting the app deletes all of it.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings — level, appearance, output mode, haptics, sleep timer.
  • Your session history — which sessions you finished, when, how long, and the answer to the closing question if you gave one. Up to the last 400.

Both are stored in the app's own container. Nothing is uploaded, backed up to a server we control, or shared with anyone. If you have iCloud Backup switched on, Apple backs up the app's container the same way it backs up every other app, under Apple's privacy policy and your own iCloud settings — we never see it.

Health

If you turn on Log to Health, the app writes finished sessions to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes. That data belongs to you and lives in Apple's Health store.

  • The app only writes. It never reads anything from Health — not sleep, not heart rate, not anything else.
  • It never writes anything except the start time and end time of a session you finished.
  • You can revoke this at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.
  • The setting is off until you turn it on.

Network

The app plays sound that is generated on your device as it plays. There are no audio files, so it works with no connection at all.

It makes network requests in exactly two situations:

  • Version check. On launch it fetches one small static file — tueyza789.github.io/deep-orbit/policy.json, the same site as this page — to see whether this version is still supported. The request carries no identifier and no information about you; like any web request it necessarily reveals your IP address to GitHub, which serves the file. If the request fails, the app carries on as normal.
  • Purchases. If you buy the full app, that transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, and we do not receive your name, email, or Apple ID.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, and the app never asks you for an email address.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Health disclaimer

This is not a medical device and it does not diagnose, treat, or prevent anything. Some people find sound like this calming and some do not. If it is not comfortable, stop.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it.
